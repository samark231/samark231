### 🚀 What I'm Building

At **Vidyayatan Technologies** I'm hands-on across **5 production projects**, contributing **300+ commits** in recent sprints:

- 🛒 **CIMS** — A multi-tenant SaaS for syncing inventory, orders & pricing across **Shopify and Amazon**. I built the **RBAC system**, **inbound webhook pipeline**, **per-channel pricing engine**, and migrated auth to **stateless JWT** (Java 21 · Spring Modulith · Next.js · Postgres · Redis).
- 🏠 **Home Review** — A property-management & tenant-review platform for the NZ market with **AI-powered video inspections**. Led the **monorepo consolidation** of 6 frontends + backend and hardened the **k8s/Helm + GitHub Actions** delivery (Spring Boot · Next.js · Capacitor · Kafka · Stripe).
- 🎓 **Vacademy** — An open-source, **AI-powered LMS** on a microservices architecture (6 Spring Boot services + a Python AI service). Worked on **SCORM compliance**, learner tracking & **Sentry observability**.
- 🏆 **MMJ Awards** — A full-stack awards platform with a public site and admin CMS, including **admin-editable dynamic nomination forms** (Spring Boot · Next.js 15 · Cloudflare Workers).
- 🌐 **IIRF Study Abroad** — A student-lead marketplace with **AES-256 encrypted leads** and credit-based unlocks. Established the **CI/CD pipeline**, project governance and **supply-chain hardening** (SHA-pinned Actions).

🌱 **Currently going deeper on:** distributed systems, Kafka event-driven design, and Kubernetes in production.
